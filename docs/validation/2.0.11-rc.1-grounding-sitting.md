# Grounding sitting — 2.0.11-rc.1 — ARC-09-C112 and C114

**What it measures:** how long a gateway Envelope takes, and what it holds, on three design prompts in
normal mode and on one of them again under Ultracode or max effort.
**Build under test:** `v2.0.11-rc.1` (`b661cf4`) · **Date:** 2026-10-08
**Model:** `claude-sonnet-5-5` · **Mode:** design-only, no instance configured.
**Run by:** the owner, in their own checkout. The Envelopes stay there; this record quotes no instance, no
user name and nothing from an Envelope but its counts.
**Measured from:** the session transcripts — each prompt's wall time from the prompt to the end of its
answer, and tool time from the tool calls in the transcript.

## The prompts

**F1 — FNOL intake.** The prompt behind T-26 in `tests/VALIDATION-TESTS.md`, which names an engagement
instead of its last sentence:

```
A P&C insurer needs first notice of loss (FNOL) intake for auto claims: the intake creates a claim from the caller's policy, assigns an adjuster by territory and sets an initial reserve. Design it. No engagement is loaded; treat this as a design-only question grounded in the docs.
```

**F2 — a custom reserve-change table.**

```
For the same insurer, build a custom table that logs every reserve change on a claim with who changed it, when, the old and new amount and the reason, and give me the table model and the Script Include.
```

**F3 — claim status in the contact centre.**

```
A policyholder calls the contact centre asking for the status of her auto claim; the agent works in the CSM Configurable Workspace. Design how the case and the claim relate and how the agent sees the claim status.
```

**The Envelope:**
- 61 KB, 184 lines;

treats a reserve row created by a flow as submitted.

## Where the time goes

Measured from the transcripts:
- **F1 to F3 in normal mode:** the tools took 4 s of about 2 min 30 s, and the corpus reads 3 s of that.
- **The Ultracode run:** the main thread's tools took 2 min 16 s of 194 min.

The time is model generation, not reading the corpus. That is why the idea of a corpus index (C116) was
parked.
